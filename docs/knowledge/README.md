# AudioScoreTool Research Knowledge Wiki

Git-managed, agent-assisted research knowledge base. Product contracts remain in `docs/`; experimental evidence lives in `docs/research/` and `benchmarks/`; accepted decisions live in `docs/decisions/`.

Read [schema](schema.md) and [wiki index](wiki/index.md). Never treat a wiki synthesis as primary evidence or an approved decision. Updates are reviewed through PRs.

Workflow: curated source → immutable raw record → evidence-tagged wiki synthesis → reproducible experiment → reviewed ADR if warranted. Do not store copyrighted music, credentials, or private data.
